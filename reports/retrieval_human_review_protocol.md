# Human review protocol for UEFA retrieval qrels

## Coach-reviewed workbook

`retrieval_review_v1.xlsx` was reviewed by two independent human coaches (`1A`
and `1B`), as confirmed by the user. The workbook contains 1,071 adjudicated
question-page labels across 42 development questions: 134 direct (grade 2),
735 partial (grade 1), 0 not relevant (grade 0), and 202 uncertain. The
workbook was not modified. A compact export at
`retrieval_workbook_review_qrels_v1.csv` contains IDs, grades, reviewer
metadata, and provenance; it does not copy full page text or quoted passages.

The candidate pool was generated from retrieval and is not exhaustive. These
ratings are genuine human coach judgments about the pages they reviewed, but
cannot establish that all relevant pages were included or measure answer
quality. They should be used to diagnose retrieval and guide candidate-pool
expansion, not alone to change production settings.

Raw exact agreement before adjudication was 937/1,071 (87.5%), including 202
pairs where both reviewers marked uncertain. Among the 869 pairs where both
gave a categorical grade, agreement was 735/869 (84.6%). These are raw exact
agreement rates, not chance-corrected reliability statistics. The spreadsheet
contains adjudicated outcomes; use the original ratings when calculating
pre-adjudication agreement.

## Reproducing the workbook diagnostics

The workbook-based ranking evaluation and backend-aligned top-3 evaluation
are generated by these scripts:

```bash
./.venv/bin/python scripts/import_retrieval_review_workbook.py /path/to/retrieval_review_v1.xlsx
./.venv/bin/python scripts/evaluate_retrieval_workbook_review_v1.py
./.venv/bin/python scripts/evaluate_backend_workbook_review.py
```

The import needs `openpyxl` from `requirements-review.txt`. The evaluation
uses grade 2 as direct support, grade 1 as partial support, grade 0 as not
relevant, and `uncertain` as unjudged. Since the candidate pool is
retrieval-generated, these scores are candidate-pool diagnostics, not
exhaustive corpus relevance metrics. The backend diagnostic measures serving
path behavior against coach-reviewed labels; it does not evaluate generated
answer quality. The exploratory tuning script reuses these labels for
selection and therefore is not an independent evaluation.

The separate AI-assisted development qrels described elsewhere in this
repository remain AI-assisted and are not human-reviewed. Do not conflate
them with this workbook.

## Page relevance task and candidate pool

The review task asks whether a UEFA report page provides evidence for a
question. It is not a vote on the best football tactic and not a review of
generated prose. StatsBomb events are not part of these page judgments.

The reviewed candidate pool contains retrieval-suggested report pages and may
omit relevant pages. Future review should add pages found through report
contents or targeted full-report search, then record provenance and whether a
page came from the existing rankers or independent search. Do not treat an
unjudged page as irrelevant.

## Rating rubric

- **2 — Direct support:** the page contains evidence that substantively answers
the question. Record the supporting passage or section.
- **1 — Partial support:** the page is materially related and useful, but
cannot answer the question on its own. Record what it supports and what is
missing.
- **0 — Not relevant:** the page does not materially support the question,
even if it shares a keyword or competition.
- **uncertain:** the page is ambiguous, too fragmentary, or cannot be judged.
Add a reason; do not force a positive or negative label.

Keep partial support separate from direct support. A question can have several
direct pages or none in the reviewed corpus/candidate pool. No direct page in
the current workbook means no direct page was identified among the reviewed
candidates; it does not prove that no report page can answer the question.

## Future review procedure

1. Have at least two reviewers rate each new question-page pair independently;
   record real reviewer backgrounds and keep each other's scores hidden.
2. Ask reviewers to search for omitted pages in the five-report corpus, not
   only rate the supplied candidates.
3. Preserve both original ratings, adjudication, supporting text, and reasons
   for uncertainty.
4. Calculate agreement before adjudication and report the denominator,
   uncertain/missing cases, and adjudication count. Do not report agreement
   after adjudication as reliability.
5. Freeze a new qrel version and keep v1/v2 question sets as held-out archives;
   do not tune on them.

## Separate answer-quality review

Page relevance is not generated-answer evaluation. A separate answer review
should score whether each answer is factually supported by cited passages,
faithful to the source, complete for the question, appropriately qualified,
and readable. Include unsupported/abstained cases and multiple tactical
question types. Keep that answer-level review distinct from retrieval
relevance metrics.
